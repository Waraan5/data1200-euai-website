What is the EU AI Act?

The EU AI Act is the world's first comprehensive legal framework for artificial intelligence.

The regulation introduces a risk-based approach to AI systems. While some AI uses are prohibited, others are subject to transparency, governance and documentation requirements.

The aim is to promote trustworthy AI while protecting fundamental rights, safety and democratic values.
